Questions

Before You Ask

Is the audit actually free?

Yes. No card, no contract and no obligation. You keep the video and the fix list whether or not you ever work with us. We do it because most brands we audit end up seeing what's possible, and some of them hire us to make it happen.

Do you need access to my Klaviyo account?

No. We audit from the outside, exactly like a customer: we subscribe, browse, abandon a cart and track every email and where it lands. If you'd like a deeper look at your flow metrics afterwards, you can share read-only access. That part is optional.

How long does it take?

Filling in the form takes about 60 seconds. Your recorded walkthrough and fix list arrive within 5 business days. Some flows only trigger after a few days, so we give them time to fire.

What if we don't send email yet?

Even better. You'll get the exact flows to build first and the order to build them in. One family farm brand we worked with went from zero email infrastructure to 1,893 orders in four months.

What happens after I get the audit?

You can hand the fix list to your team, or book a call and we'll walk you through it. If you want us to implement it, we'll talk about that on the call. No pressure either way.
