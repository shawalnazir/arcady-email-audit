What do I actually get?

A recorded video walkthrough of your email program and a fix list ranked by revenue impact. We cover flows, campaigns, deliverability, list growth, design and how much of your revenue email drives today.

Why email, and not more ads?

Ads get more expensive every year. Email sells to people who already know you, at almost no cost per send. Our strongest programs drive 30% or more of total revenue, without adding a dollar to ad spend.

Will this work for my store?

It works best for e-commerce brands doing $30K or more a month with steady traffic and customers. Below that, you'll still get your audit, but the gains are smaller until volume grows.

How is Arcady different from other agencies?

Email and retention is our core, and we have 58+ published case studies to show for it. Several of our clients came to us after other agencies, and many have stayed for years.

Do you need my Klaviyo login?

No. We audit from the outside, like a customer: we subscribe, browse, abandon a cart and track every email and where it lands. Read-only access for a deeper look is optional.

What happens after I apply?

If your store is a fit, you'll pick a time for a walkthrough call right away. Either way, your audit arrives within 5 business days, and you keep it whether or not we ever work together.
