Meta Campaign Plan

Who the free email audit ads target, how the campaigns are structured and scaled, which events go to Meta, and how every lead lands in GoHighLevel (or any CRM).

1 · Targeting

Three Audiences to Test

One broad audience, one interest stack and one lookalike, tested side by side in the first 10 days.

All ad sets: United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia · ages 25–65 · Advantage+ placements · English
Excluded everywhere: existing clients (customer list) and anyone who submitted the form in the last 30 days
Ad set 1

Broad

No interests or lookalikes. The creative does the targeting: every ad calls out "e-commerce brands doing $30K+ a month" in the first line, so Meta learns who responds.

Ad set 2

Store Owners + Shopify

Business owners and store owners, narrowed to people interested in Shopify and e-commerce tools.

Include any of
Small business ownersFacebook Page adminsBusiness owner (job title)
And narrow to any of
ShopifyKlaviyoE-commerceWooCommerceBigCommerceRechargeOnline store

Meta renames and removes interests often, so each one gets checked in Ads Manager at build.

Ad set 3

Lookalike of Arcady's Prospects

  • Seed: one list of everyone Arcady has spoken to: past leads, booked calls, proposals, clients, newsletter subscribers
  • Size: 1,000+ people for a reliable lookalike (minimum 100)
  • Lookalike: 1–3% per country. Value-based if client revenue data is available
  • Later: a second lookalike from the funnel's own qualified leads and booked calls

3 · Tracking

Events Sent to Meta

Every event fires from the browser (pixel) and from the server (Conversions API) with the same event ID, so Meta counts it once. The server copy still gets through when ad blockers or iOS privacy settings block the pixel.

EventWhen it firesWhereUsed for
PageViewEvery page loadAll funnel pagesRetargeting audiences and funnel drop-off
ViewContentThe VSL page loads/vsl/Retargeting people who saw the offer but didn't apply
LeadPhases 1–2Any form is submitted (opt-in or VSL application)/ and /vsl/Main optimisation event. Sent with the funnel name, revenue band and email challenge
QualifiedLeadThe applicant says $30K+/month (custom event)/ and /vsl/Judging audience quality, reporting, and a lookalike seed later
SchedulePhase 3A call is bookedConfirmation page + booking webhookOptimisation once there's enough volume
PurchaseThe opportunity is marked Won in GHL or any CRM (offline, server only)GHL / any CRMShows which ads produce clients, not just leads. Sent with the deal value

Customer data sent with each event

Email, phone, first and last name, country and an external ID, all hashed (SHA-256), plus the fbp / fbc cookies, IP address and browser. More matching data means better attribution and better optimisation.

Before launch

Every event gets tested with a Test Event Code in Events Manager. Target an Event Match Quality of 7+ on Lead and Schedule.

Where the emails run

The 3-email follow-up sequence runs in Klaviyo, as built. The form sends each lead to Klaviyo and the CRM at the same time: Klaviyo handles nurture emails, GHL (or the CRM) handles the pipeline, booking and reminders. Anyone tagged call-booked leaves the email sequence.
