## Who they are
- E-commerce / DTC marketing agency, built on Shopify. Core is **email and retention (Klaviyo)**. Advertorials, CRO and paid ads support it.
- Hero: "Grow *profit.* Not just clicks."
- Sub: "Built for brands that need their next agency to be their last."
- Team works across time zones. Only names seen: Tarun and Claudia (in a Sunchill testimonial). No founder or about page.

## Services
1. **Lifecycle Marketing:** email, SMS, loyalty, subscriptions (LTV).
2. **Conversion Optimization (CRO):** landing page strategy, UX audits, testing ("without more ad spend").
3. **Advertising Services:** Meta, TikTok, Google, native ("scale profit and feed owned channels").
4. **Advertorials:** long-form pre-sell content. "Your story sells before they even click 'buy.'"

Partners / stack: Shopify, Klaviyo, Yotpo, Recharge, Black Crow AI.
