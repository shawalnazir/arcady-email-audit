## 6. Why it works (2:36–3:47)

Now, if you want to know why email works so well, it's simple. There are really only two ways to grow an online store: get more new customers, or get more from the customers you already have.

Most brands only do the first one. They pour more money into Meta and Google. And every year, those ads get more expensive. You pay more for every click, more for every first purchase, and the moment you stop spending, the sales stop too.

*B-roll: rising cost-per-click chart*

Email is the opposite. Those subscribers and customers are already yours. You've already paid to get them. Every email you send costs almost nothing, and a good flow keeps selling twenty-four seven, whether you're running ads or not.

That's why our strongest programs drive thirty percent or more of total revenue from email. Little English gets forty-two percent of its sales from email. Same traffic, same ad budget, a lot more revenue.

The problem is most brands set up a few flows years ago and never looked at them again. That's where the money's hiding, and that's exactly what the audit finds.
