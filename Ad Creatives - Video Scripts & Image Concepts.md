# Ad Creatives: Video Scripts & Image Concepts (Arcady Media)

Creatives for the Meta ad, to run with the primary texts, headlines and descriptions in `Meta Ad Copy - Free Email Audit.md`.

- **Video 1:** founder talking to camera, 9:16, about 45 seconds
- **Video 2:** text on video, 9:16, about 12 seconds, no speaker needed
- **Image 1:** hand holding a card, 4:5
- **Image 2:** "your options" card, 4:5

Brand: Arcady blue `#5176F9`, navy `#141722`, white. Font: Urbanist (or any clean geometric sans).

---

## Video 1: Founder to Camera (9:16, ~45s)

**Format:** Tarun (or whoever presents) talks straight to camera, seated, in a dark, warm-lit room. Bold burned-in captions in the lower third, with key words highlighted in Arcady blue. A **pinned banner** sits at the top for the whole video. Cutaways to proof on screen.

**Pinned top banner (whole video):** `Free Email Audit for E-commerce Brands`

| Time | Spoken | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:04 | If you run an e-commerce brand doing thirty thousand a month or more, give me fifteen seconds. | Tight shot. Caption highlight: **$30K+ a month** |
| 0:04–0:09 | Most brands grow by buying more ads. But those ads cost more every year. | B-roll: rising cost line on a chart. Caption highlight: **cost more** |
| 0:09–0:18 | Your email list is different. Those customers are already yours, but most brands set up their flows once and never touch them again. | Back to camera. Caption highlight: **already yours** |
| 0:18–0:28 | So we'll audit yours for free: every flow, your inbox placement, and a fix list ranked by revenue. No logins needed. | Text pops in one by one: "✓ Every flow" "✓ Inbox placement" "✓ Fix list ranked by revenue" |
| 0:28–0:36 | It's the same work that built Vitality four point nine million dollars in email revenue. | Full-screen proof cards: "Vitality: **$4.9M** in email revenue" then "Enhanced Scents: **$200K → $1.2M/mo**" |
| 0:36–0:45 | Want to know what your list is really worth? Tap the button below for your free audit. | Back to camera, pointing down. End card: "Free · No logins · Delivered in 5 business days" |

**About 105 spoken words**, so there's room for pauses and the proof cards. Pairs best with the **short-form** primary text.

**Shooting notes:**
- Same setup as the VSL, so both can be filmed in one session.
- Film 3 hook variations for testing:
  - "Give me fifteen seconds and I'll show you where your email is leaking revenue."
  - "Most Shopify brands get under 20% of their revenue from email. Here's why."
  - "Stop buying the same customer twice."
- Captions: white, bold, 2–4 words at a time, centred in the lower third, safe area kept clear of the Reels UI (keep the bottom 20% and the right edge free).

## Video 2: Text on Video (9:16, ~12s, no speaker)

**Format:** one slow, cinematic background clip with a block of text on top that appears line by line. Light music, no voice-over. Cheap to make and quick to test new angles.

**Background clip (generate in Higgsfield, 5–10s, loop or extend):**
> Vertical 9:16 cinematic video, slow push-in. A tidy desk at night in a small modern studio office. A smartphone lies face up and keeps lighting up with new order notifications stacking on the lock screen. Behind it, a laptop shows a blurred email marketing dashboard with rising bar charts. Warm desk lamp, cool blue light from the screens, shallow depth of field, soft film grain. Realistic, calm, premium. No readable brand names or logos.

**Text overlay (centred, appears line by line):**

| Time | Text |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | **Your customers already bought once.** |
| 0:01 | **Your emails should make them buy again.** |
| 0:03 | *Free email audit for brands doing $30K+/month:* |
| 0:04 | + We check every flow: welcome, cart, post-purchase, winback |
| 0:06 | + We test whether your emails land in the inbox |
| 0:07 | + You get a fix list ranked by revenue |
| 0:09 | *The same work behind $4.9M in email revenue for Vitality.* |
| 0:10 | **No logins. No cost. Tap the button below.** |

**Text style:** headline lines in a bold serif or Urbanist ExtraBold, white with a soft shadow. "+" markers in Arcady blue. Body lines smaller, in italic for the qualifier and proof lines. Hold the final frame for 2 seconds.

Pairs with either primary text. Make 3–4 versions with different background clips (packing station, unboxing on a kitchen counter, inbox on a phone) and the same text.

## Image 1: Hand Holding a Card (4:5, 1080×1350)

**Concept:** a photo of a hand holding up a white card with a short, punchy message in marker-style lettering. It looks like a real photo, not an ad, which helps it stand out in the feed.

**Card text:**
- Line 1 (black marker): **YOU DIDN'T BUILD A BRAND TO PAY FOR THE SAME CUSTOMER TWICE.**
- Line 2 (black marker on blue highlighter stripes): **YOUR EMAILS SHOULD BRING THEM BACK.**
- Small line (clean print): Free email audit for e-commerce brands.
- Bottom left (small, bold): ARCADY MEDIA
- Bottom right (handwritten with an arrow): Get your free audit →

**Higgsfield prompt:**
> Square-ish 4:5 product-style photo. A woman's hand holds up a slightly tilted white square card against a dark navy-to-deep-blue gradient background (#141722 to #2a3a8c). The card has bold, uppercase, hand-drawn black marker lettering that reads "YOU DIDN'T BUILD A BRAND TO PAY FOR THE SAME CUSTOMER TWICE." Below it, two lines highlighted with bright blue highlighter stripes (#5176F9) read "YOUR EMAILS SHOULD BRING THEM BACK." Under that, in small clean sans-serif print: "Free email audit for e-commerce brands." Bottom left corner in small bold letters: "ARCADY MEDIA". Bottom right in casual handwriting with a drawn arrow: "Get your free audit →". Soft studio lighting, realistic skin and paper texture, sharp focus on the card, subtle shadow, minimal composition.

**Tip:** image models often misspell longer text. If the lettering comes out wrong, generate the same scene with a **blank card**, then add the text in Canva with a marker-style font (e.g. Permanent Marker) and a blue highlight shape.

## Notes

- **No guarantee claims:** none of the creatives say "or you don't pay". The risk reversal is "free, no logins".
- **Numbers:** Vitality $4.9M and Enhanced Scents $200K → $1.2M/mo are from Arcady's published case studies.
- **Hook test idea:** the "Most Shopify brands get under 20% of their revenue from email" hook is an industry claim, not an Arcady number. Use it only if Arcady is comfortable with it, or swap it for one of the other two.
- **Formats to export:** videos at 1080×1920 (Reels/Stories), images at 1080×1350 (feed). Make a 1:1 crop of the images for right-column placements if needed.
