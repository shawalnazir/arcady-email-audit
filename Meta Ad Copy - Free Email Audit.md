## Notes

- **Sender:** the long form is signed "Tarun", to match the follow-up emails. Confirm Tarun is the right person to sign as.
- **Proof:** every number is from arcadymedia.com case studies. The Klaviyo line paraphrases the partner quote on their site ("Arcady Media's accounts performs 30x better than the average Klaviyo ROI").
- **No guarantee or fake scarcity:** the offer is free, so the risk reversal is "keep the fix list either way". There's no "spots filling fast" because there's no real limit.
- **First line matters most:** Meta shows about 125 characters before "See more". Both versions put the audience ($30K+/month e-commerce) and the promise in that space.
- **Claims that must match the landing pages:** $30K+/month, "within 5 business days", "no logins", video walkthrough + fix list. If the follow-up emails stay as "audit on a 30-minute call", one side needs to change.
